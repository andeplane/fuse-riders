# City concepts

Concept boards for future city types. Only Fusetown (concept 5, the harbour town) is implemented: its label-free
picture is `public/fuse-lane/cities/fusetown.jpg` and its streets are traced in `src/game/data/cities/fusetown.ts`.
Another city is another file like that one, plus its picture in the art table.

- `option-1.jpg`: winding streets round a clock-tower square, with parks and car parks.
- `option-2.jpg`: a dense downtown on a roundabout round the clock tower.
- `option-3.jpg`: a hillside town of terraces, steps and switchbacks.
- `option-4.jpg`: looping, figure-of-eight streets round a town square and car parks.

The baked-in labels are placeholders from the concept stage; a city's real picture has none (the game draws the names).
