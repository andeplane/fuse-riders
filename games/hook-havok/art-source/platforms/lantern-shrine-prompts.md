# Lantern shrine generation record

Generated using the built-in ImageGen tool. The first output had a painted dark background and uneven quadrant packing and was rejected. The correction is the runtime source; exact source dimensions, alpha and browser evidence are recorded in the phase document.

## Initial generation

```text
Create a production game environment sprite atlas for Hook Havok, original hand-painted gothic fantasy with bold dark ink contours, desaturated blue slate stone, antique brass, muted plum cloth, amber candles and tiny violet mushrooms. Genuine transparent background PNG. EXACT 2 columns by 2 rows evenly sized cells, all assets separately isolated inside their own cell with generous transparent margins. No text, no grids, no backgrounds, no ground shadows, no characters. Cell top left: a wide horizontal side-view stone platform, perfectly flat continuous level walkable top, layered chipped ashlar stones, carved scrollwork lower lip, dark iron corbels underneath, about 4:1 width to height, clean end caps, NO props above the top. Cell top right: a second distinct wide horizontal flat-topped stone platform same proportions and scale, slightly different stonework with tiny ivy hanging beneath, no props above top. Cell bottom left: tall narrow hanging antique brass shrine ornament, pointed iron arch and weathered plum tapestry with original simple gold lantern emblem, two short attachment chains at its top, about 1:2 width to height, fully contained. Cell bottom right: low wide small arrangement of three warm lit ivory candles with melted wax, a few tiny violet mushrooms and moss, about 2:1 width to height. Clear consistent side elevation for a 2D platform game, rich detailed texture and painterly brushwork that remains readable at small size. Background must be actually transparent alpha; no checkerboard painting, no halo or baked diffuse lighting around objects. Keep each object in central 75 percent of its quadrant, no touching cell edges.
```

## Correction

Reference: the first generation, used solely to preserve the four original drawings.

```text
Correct this production sprite atlas for game use. Keep these SAME four illustrated assets and their detailed gothic style. Remove ALL background pixels, gradients, shadows and glow so the empty space is genuine transparent alpha. Repack as SQUARE canvas with EXACT uniform 2 columns and 2 rows. Top left cell stone ledge A, top right cell stone ledge B, bottom left cell full hanging shrine banner including chains, bottom right cell full candles and mushrooms. Scale each entire object to fit WITHIN central 75% of its OWN quadrant with generous transparent padding on all sides. Banner must be entirely below horizontal halfway line. Platforms must have flat perfectly horizontal tops. No object, chain, flame, glow or foliage may cross into another quadrant. No new artwork, no text, no background, no glow outside the candle flame itself. PNG genuine transparency.
```
