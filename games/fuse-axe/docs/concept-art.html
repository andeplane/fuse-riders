FUSE AXECONCEPT ART · ASHEN VILLAGE

A co-op beat-em-up in the spirit of Golden Axe II. Everything on this page is drawn live from palette-indexed pixel rows ({ w, h, ax, ay, palette, rows }, where ax, ay is the feet anchor) and a few procedural backdrop layers, on the native 320×180 grid, scaled by whole numbers with nearest-neighbour.

1Key scene · Ashen Village

Stage 1 at dusk, mid-wave: Brakka's combo connects, Gorm swings through a chop, Rhea holds the back line and a sack imp flees, dropping a pot. HUD strip on top (portrait, name, health, pots, lives; two open seats) and the GO marker at the right edge. Shown at 3× and at 1×.

2Heroes

Idle and attack frames at 4×. Light from the upper left, 1-px dark outlines, three-shade ramps per material.

3Enemies, mounts and the boss

Same 4× scale, so sizes compare directly with the heroes. The ravager tiers are one sprite with a three-colour palette swap.

4Magic

One vignette per spell, 112×64 at 3×. Magic gets the platform's neon: each caster glows in their spell's colour.

5Palette

The shared master palette. Sprite rows use these one-character keys; swaps (tiers, duplicate heroes, damage flash) are palette overrides. Ramps run light, base, shadow; shadows lean violet and lights lean gold.
