## External qualification and accepted limitations

Physical iPhone/Android touch, GPU/thermal behavior, lock/background/bfcache and Wi-Fi/mobile transitions require access not established by these desktop tests. Verified OS/IP packet-loss/congestion and wire-byte counters require a different impairment layer; pre-SCTP message dropping is intentionally adversarial application testing. Missing hardware evidence is an unverified claim, not itself proof of a product defect.

A trusted foreground browser host, direct-connect NAT limitations and possible safe cancellation of a shot during interruption are documented product constraints. The user-approved direct-only decision excludes gameplay WSS fallback and cross-person host migration; those must not reappear as invented completion blockers. Provider residual IAM scope and an unexecuted compatible rollback rehearsal remain disclosed operational evidence limits, not automatically new feature requirements.

ADR 032 labels its broad numeric matrix proposed. This audit does not promote every proposed row into a hard requirement or waive it. The user explicitly requested real benchmarks, responsive motion and good online behavior; report the measurements and reviewed response target honestly, distinguish external evidence from fixable defects, and avoid presenting measured desktop acceptance as a guarantee for every phone or network.
