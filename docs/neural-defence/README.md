# Neural Defence

Neural Defence is a real-time territory RTS prototype: grow a connected network from a brain, mine **Biomass** and **Insight**, research, and direct a fixed pool of attack particles. Phase 0 is implemented locally for draft [PR #409](https://github.com/andeplane/fuse-riders/pull/409), including the headless core, Fuse Riders' shared neon/pixel UI and individual generated world sprites. Final verification and the user's visual/play acceptance remain open; no competitive balance or production claim follows from the current tests.

The first playable modes are a one-player sandbox without AI and a scripted combat lab with one opposing network and **one test tower**. The menu has **New game** and **Settings**; Settings currently offers reduced motion. Audio is not implemented. Each player has one builder for queued construction and 128 separate reusable attack particles. Growth, Excitation and Conduction research are independent. The engine supports 1–4 owners headlessly; online multiplayer UI is later work.

The battlefield uses the full width between a thin resource bar and a compact bottom command dock. Select a hex to inspect it, build or change its attack priority. Build, Research and Log replace the command card in place; A or Escape returns to the main commands. Drag the battlefield to pan and scroll to zoom around the pointer. On phones, drag with one finger or pinch with two to zoom. Arrow keys move the selected hex and bring it into view; Enter queues a neuron on open ground. Camera controls only change presentation, never simulation coordinates or timing.

The bottom-right icon command card mirrors the keyboard: **Q W E / A S D**. Main commands are Q Build, W Research, E Log and A cancel selected construction. Build contains Q Neuron, W test Tower, A Back and S cancel selected construction. Research contains Q Growth, W Excitation, E Conduction, A Back and S cancel research. Unused slots stay empty; larger catalogs receive a D More button automatically. There are no Fit map or +/− buttons.

Clock overlays show construction and research progress on the corresponding command and its parent category. Hover or focus a command to inspect its requirements; tap grey buttons on phones for the same explanation. The [shared command catalog](COMMANDS.md) supplies costs and structured missing requirements to both simulation and presentation. Legal construction plans remain queueable while waiting for resources or support. Grey commands never dispatch actions, and typing/slider controls keep their normal keyboard input.
