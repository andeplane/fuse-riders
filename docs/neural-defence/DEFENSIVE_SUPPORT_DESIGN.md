# Next balance experiment: supplied defensive support

Status: the original hypothesis below is now implemented as a **rules-7 balance candidate**, described in [PROTECTION.md](PROTECTION.md). Broad balance remains unproven. The earlier rules-6 default-arena trial gave Pressure a win against every other opening and Defensive no non-mirror wins.

Bastion currently has only range-one offense while every specialist weapon can attack from farther away. Its intended defensive role needs to contribute when enemies stand outside that range. Increasing all of its damage or range would blur existing weapon roles.

Evaluate a small, catalog-defined protective field powered by the same finite particle pool. A connected, supplied Bastion would spend stationed particles to absorb part of incoming damage to itself and adjacent friendly structures or paid sites. Empty or disconnected Bastions provide no protection. Heavy attacks should exhaust protection faster, and maintaining the field must compete with supplying guns. It must not become an independent, free regenerating health pool.

The experiment needs explicit deterministic allocation when several targets share a protector, honest damage/absorption outcomes, no duplicate protection of an upgrade source and its scaffold, checkpoint validation, and AI support priorities that actually deliver ammunition. All graphics must follow real absorption outcomes: a brief raised shield arc and ground reflection, rather than a permanently opaque bubble over the artwork.

Test isolated supplied/empty/disconnected cases and shared-budget simultaneous attacks first. Then compare the complete strategy matrix against the rules-6 baseline, retaining losing matchups, mirror timeouts and positional differences. Keep the feature only if it adds counterplay without producing an unbreakable defensive economy. The ongoing broader goal also still requires stronger dimensional battlefield presentation and animated playtest inspection.
