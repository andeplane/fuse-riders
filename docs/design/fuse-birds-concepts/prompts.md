## 01-neon-burrow

Use case: ui-mockup. Generate one polished 16:9 landscape mock gameplay screenshot for FUSE BIRDS, an original 2–5 player turn-based slingshot artillery game mixing pull-and-release aiming, classic Tank Wars terrain and Worms-style shootable supply crates. The attached Riders screenshot is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: preserve its dark navy background, subtle fine blue grid, cyan and magenta neon, pixel title typography, thin luminous frames, compact dark player cards and bright round pickup icon styling. Change the game to a SIDE-ON 2D artillery battlefield, not top-down trails. This must look like a sibling game to that reference, not generic Angry Birds art. Four small original expressive bird characters with beaks in cyan, magenta, lime and orange stand on separate uneven terrain perches. Show a short dotted rising aim arc pointing toward a wooden supply crate on a ledge. Another wooden crate descends under a small parachute. Crates have a luminous medical plus or ammo icon. Wide readable cutaway destructible terrain with hills, a crater, arch and gaps, a dark blue water hazard along the bottom. Birds are small relative to the landscape but recognizable. Crisp practical screenshot composition; no monitor frame, no poster, no giant mascots, no copied franchise characters, no photorealism, no large explanatory text. Pixel headings with readable small interface text; controlled bloom so silhouettes and aim remain crisp.
